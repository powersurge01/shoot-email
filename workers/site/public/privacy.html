Privacy Policy

Effective July 30, 2026

Shoot Email is an experimental, open-source email service designed for AI-agent workflows. This policy explains what information the hosted Shoot Email service processes and why.

Information used for sign-in

Shoot Email uses Auth0 as its authorization provider and Google as an optional identity provider. Google may provide Auth0 with basic profile information such as your name, profile image, email address, and stable Google account identifier. Shoot Email uses a validated Auth0 identifier to associate you with an internal mailbox.

Shoot Email does not request access to your Gmail mailbox, Google contacts, Google Drive files, or other Google application content.

Email and service data

The service may store:

  • Your generated Shoot Email address and sender display name.
  • Email sender, recipient, subject, plain-text body, provider message identifiers, and relevant timestamps.
  • Message delivery, processing, acknowledgement, and error status.
  • Pseudonymous identity and conversation identifiers required to map requests to the correct mailbox.
  • Rate-limit, abuse-prevention, and operational diagnostic records.

How information is used

Information is processed to:

  • Authenticate users and maintain stable mailbox identity.
  • Send, receive, retrieve, and acknowledge email messages.
  • Preserve conversation history and support reliable retries.
  • Apply sending limits, prevent abuse, and protect service security.
  • Diagnose failures and operate the hosted service.

Service providers

The hosted service relies on Auth0 for authorization, Google when a user selects Google sign-in, Cloudflare for email and compute services, and Neon for managed PostgreSQL storage. These providers process data under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Retention and security

Messages remain available as conversation history after processing unless removed through an administrative operation. Shoot Email uses scoped OAuth access, encrypted network transport, provider-managed infrastructure, and application-level abuse controls. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your responsibilities

Do not send secrets or sensitive personal information unless the recipient and the AI agent handling the message are appropriate for that information. Email content is untrusted external input and may be processed by an AI system chosen by the user.

Account access and deletion requests

During the private beta, access may be disabled or revoked by the service operator. On a verified deletion request, Shoot Email can irreversibly anonymize stored mailbox data by removing external identity links, sessions, correspondent records, embeddings, and message content. Previously assigned mailbox aliases are retained only as disabled, non-reusable tombstones to prevent reassignment.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions or data requests, open a private contact request through the project owner's contact information on GitHub. Do not include passwords, OAuth tokens, or sensitive email content in a public issue.

Policy changes

This policy may change as the service develops. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.
